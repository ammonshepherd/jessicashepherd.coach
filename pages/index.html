About me

Hi! I'm Jessica. I'm so honored to help you achieve your goals.

We're going to have so much fun! I'm a lot like you. A woman with a body in our modern society trying to survive thrive. Women are socialized to have certain ideas about what a body "should" look like-our hair, face, skin, weight, size all "should" look and stay that way. Our modern information age is such a blessing with SO much info, but sometimes it's too much thrown at us, and we're told we "have" to control it all-BMI, aging, mental health, gut health, muscle mass,sun exposure, water intake, blood sugar, fiber, protein, etc etc etc.

There's so much chatter out there about what the BEST thing to do is. There's SO much information, it's hard to sort through all of it, and harder to get ourselves to stick to our goals and muster the will to keep going day in and out.

I'm a mother of 6 kids, so I know busy. And as a woman who has birthed 6 kids, I know about body changing-hormones, sleep deprivation, aging and mental effects of all of it. I know different kids stretch you in different ways (emotionally and physically-yes, I've got the stretch marks to show off, too). I have 20 years of fitness experience, 10 years as a fitness instructor, including years of wellness coaching, all helping me through my life struggles. I am also a certified Life Coach through The Life Coach School.

Coaching has made the difference. Through it all, I've learned to love my body. That doesn't mean I'm the exact weight or size I (used to) wish I was. I've just learned to love me into change, instead of hating or loathing myself into change. And the amazing ripple effects are that I love my husband, my children, my community, the world, everything about life more. My parenting is better, my relationships are better, my health is better, my results are better. Coaching is my mental gym, which has transformed my body and life into the amazing experience I've always dreamed of.

My mental gym will get you to the physical gym. 😀 I will guide you along your health journey to the best version of you. Come with me! 🧡
